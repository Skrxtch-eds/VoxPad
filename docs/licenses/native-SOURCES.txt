VoxPad native dependencies

FFmpeg is used through PyAV 19.0.2 (BSD-3-Clause).
The bundled libavutil reports: LGPL version 3 or later.
License texts: native-FFmpeg-COPYING.LGPLv3 and native-FFmpeg-COPYING.GPLv3.
FFmpeg source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
PyAV source and wheel build scripts: https://github.com/PyAV-Org/PyAV/tree/v19.0.2
The FFmpeg shared DLLs live in _internal/av.libs and may be replaced with
interface-compatible builds. Codec DLLs have their own upstream licenses.

Source: https://github.com/PortAudio/portaudio

License: native-PortAudio-LICENSE.txt; binaries are in _internal/_sounddevice_data.

Tcl/Tk: https://github.com/tcltk/tcl and https://github.com/tcltk/tk
License: native-Tcl-LICENSE.txt and _internal/_tk_data/license.terms.

libsndfile and its codec dependencies: see
licenses/soundfile/licensing/license_notes.md and
licenses/soundfile/_soundfile_data/COPYING.
Source: https://github.com/libsndfile/libsndfile

pystray 0.19.5: LGPLv3, https://github.com/moses-palmer/pystray
License: licenses/pystray/pystray-0.19.5.dist-info/COPYING.LGPL and COPYING.
The source distribution is available from https://pypi.org/project/pystray/0.19.5/
